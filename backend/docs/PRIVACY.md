**BY USING OUR PLATFORM, YOU EXPRESSLY CONSENT TO OUR COLLECTION, USE, AND DISCLOSURE OF YOUR INFORMATION AS DESCRIBED IN THIS POLICY.**

## 2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

### 2.1 Account & Personal Information
- **Basic Information**: Name, phone number, email address
- **Identification**: Government-issued ID numbers, ID document copies
- **Property Information**: Unit number, building name, address, lease information
- **Profile Data**: Profile photographs, avatars
- **Device Tokens**: FCM tokens for push notifications

### 2.2 Financial & Payment Information
- **Bank Details**: Bank name, account number, account name
- **Mobile Money**: M-Pesa phone number
- **Payout Settings**: Payout percentages, earnings data
- **Payment Records**: Transaction history, payment amounts, payment status
- **Rent Information**: Rent amounts, deposit amounts, rent balance, rent status
- **Revenue Data**: Platform fees, tenant share, admin earnings

### 2.3 Vehicle & Transportation Information
- **License Plate Numbers**: Vehicle registration details
- **Vehicle Descriptions**: Make, model, color
- **Vehicle Photos**: If captured by security

### 2.4 Visitor & Delivery Information
- **Visitor Data**: Name, phone, ID number, purpose of visit, host information
- **Visitor Photos**: Check-in and check-out photographs
- **Delivery Data**: Courier name, phone, company, tracking number
- **Parcel Information**: Description, weight, photos
- **Delivery Signatures**: Digital signatures on delivery

### 2.5 Security & Access Information
- **Access Logs**: Entry/exit timestamps, access methods, duration of visits
- **Gate Camera Feeds**: URLs and access to security camera streams
- **QR Codes**: Generated unique codes for visitors and deliveries
- **Biometric Data**: Facial recognition and photos where applicable
- **Location Data**: GPS coordinates of properties

### 2.6 Maintenance & Issue Reports
- **Issue Descriptions**: Text descriptions of problems
- **Issue Photos**: Photographs of maintenance issues
- **Guest Submissions**: Name, phone, unit number from non-users

### 2.7 Automatically Collected Information
- **Device Information**: IP addresses, browser types, device identifiers
- **Usage Data**: Pages visited, features used, interactions
- **Timestamps**: All creation and update times

### 2.8 Property & Building Information
- **Building Details**: Building names, codes, floor plans
- **Unit Information**: Unit numbers, types, occupancy status, rent amounts
- **Security Configuration**: Gate locations, camera placements
- **Amenities**: Available building amenities
- **Rules**: Visitor policies, check-in/out times
